Lab Lead at VOSS, final year EXCS.

I started this lab because open source looked closed from the outside to me too.
It seemed like something other people were already inside of, and there was no
obvious door. There isn't a door. You open a pull request, someone reads it, and
you are in. That is genuinely the whole thing, and nobody told me, so I am
telling you.

Almost everything I know I learned by reading other people's code and having mine
picked apart in review. Outside VOSS I am the top contributor to
[Memori](https://github.com/MemoriLabs/Memori), an agent memory layer with 15.6k
stars, the fifth contributor to [Swarms](https://github.com/kyegomez/swarms), a
multi-agent framework with 7k, and I have a merged pull request in
[OpenAI's gpt-oss](https://github.com/openai/gpt-oss). Every one of those started
exactly where you are right now: one small pull request into a stranger's
repository, not knowing whether it would be accepted.

VERP exists because our attendance lived in a spreadsheet and I wanted to find
out whether students could just fix that. Turns out we can.

Ask me anything, in the issue thread. No question here is too basic, and I would
far rather answer the same one twenty times than have you quietly give up.
